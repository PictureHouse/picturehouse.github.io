## What the app does

The app helps you focus by blocking the websites and apps you choose. It offers:

- **Site and app blocking**: blocks the websites and apps (or app categories) you select for the period you choose.
- **Focus Mode**: save the sites and apps to block as presets and run timed focus sessions. While a session runs, the remaining time is shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- **Scheduled focus**: starts and ends focus sessions automatically on the days and times you set.
- **Focus history**: shows statistics and a record of your focus sessions.
- **Hidden list**: hides blocked items from the list and protects them with Face ID or other device authentication.

Blocking is performed through Apple's Screen Time frameworks, which require you to grant Screen Time access.

## About block periods

- **While the block period you selected is running, the item cannot be deleted inside the app.** This is intentional and exists to prevent impulsive unblocking.
- When the period ends, the block lifts automatically, and you can then delete the item or extend its period.
- If you choose **"Permanent," the item can never be unblocked or deleted within the app.** Please choose carefully.
- Deleting the app or revoking Screen Time access causes iOS to lift the restrictions the app set. The app cannot prevent this, and it does not guarantee that every workaround is blocked.

## About Focus Mode

- You can end a running focus session early after confirming; it is then recorded as "stopped."
- Scheduled focus sessions are started by iOS at the times you set. A scheduled session does not start while another focus session is already running.
- Depending on device state and iOS policies, sessions may start or end slightly off the set time.

## Free plan and Plus

- The app's core features are free. The free plan limits the number of sites and apps you can add, the block periods you can choose, the length of focus sessions, and how much history you can view; the app shows the current limits.
- Plus removes these limits and adds features such as blocking app categories, scheduled focus, and the hidden list.
- When Plus ends, Plus-only features are no longer available. Blocks you already set stay in place until their periods end, hidden items appear in the main list again, and scheduled focus no longer runs.
