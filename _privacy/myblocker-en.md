My Blocker ("the app") is built to work without collecting your personal information. It processes only what it needs to function, and only on your device.

## Summary

- The app has **no sign-up or login**, and does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
- Your block lists, focus presets, schedules, focus history, and settings are **stored only on your device** and are never sent to the developer. The developer cannot access them.
- The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and does not sell or share your information with third parties.

## Information stored on your device

The following is kept only in the app's private storage (App Group) on your device, shared by the app and its extensions (widget and Live Activity, Share extension, Screen Time monitor):

- **Site blocking**: the website domains you block, with when each was added, its block duration, and whether it is hidden
- **App blocking**: identifiers representing the apps and app categories you chose to block (see "Use of Screen Time" below), with when each was added, its block duration, and whether it is hidden
- **Focus presets**: each preset's name and the domains and app identifiers it blocks, and the preset you last selected
- **Scheduled focus**: weekdays, start and end times, the preset to use, and whether the schedule is on
- **Focus history**: preset name, start and end times, planned and actual focus time, and whether the session was completed
- **App settings**: the display language, and whether Plus is active (the last known status, cached so features work)

This information never leaves your device and is **removed when you delete the app.**

## Use of Screen Time

The app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to block websites and apps.

- When you select apps or categories to block, the app receives only **opaque tokens** provided by Apple. These tokens do not reveal app names or usage history.
- The app does not collect your screen time, app usage history, or browsing history.
- So that scheduled focus sessions and focus timers start and end on time, iOS may run the app's Screen Time monitor extension on your device while the app is closed. Nothing is sent off the device in this process either.

## Lock Screen display (Live Activity)

While a focus session runs, the **preset name and remaining time** appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The Lock Screen can be seen by others without unlocking your device; if you prefer, you can turn off Live Activities for the app in iOS Settings.

## Clipboard

When you go to add a blocked site, the app checks whether the clipboard contains a web address and offers it as a suggestion. iOS may ask whether to allow pasting. Clipboard contents are **used on your device only to show the suggestion** and are not stored or sent anywhere unless you add the site.

## Share extension

When you choose the app from the share menu in Safari or another app, the shared web address is passed to the app to prefill the add-site screen. This happens entirely on your device.

## Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID)

Biometric authentication or your device passcode is used to open your hidden list. Authentication is performed by iOS; the app receives **only a success or failure result.** Your biometric data is never made available to the app.

## Information sent off the device

The app communicates externally only in these cases:

- **Site icons (favicons):** to show icons in your blocked site list, the app requests each domain's icon from Google's favicon service. The blocked domain and general network information such as your IP address may be sent to Google, which handles it under Google's privacy policy.
- **Purchases and subscriptions:** payments are handled by Apple's App Store. The app never collects or stores payment details and only checks your purchase and subscription status with Apple, which handles payment information under Apple's privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

- All information stays on your device until you delete it or delete the app.
- Blocked items, presets, and schedules can be deleted in the app. However, **items whose block period is still running cannot be deleted until it ends, and permanent blocks cannot be deleted in the app.**
- Deleting the app removes all information, including your focus history.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will be provided with an app update.
