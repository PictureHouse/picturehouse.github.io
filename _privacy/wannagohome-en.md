**집가고싶다** (the "App") takes your privacy seriously.
The App has **no sign-up and no server of its own.** Everything the App creates is stored only on your device and is
never transmitted anywhere outside it, including to the developer.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

The App stores and uses the following information **only on your device**.

- **Workplace information** — the name, latitude and longitude, and arrival detection radius of the workplace you pin on the map
- **Leave-time information** — today's leave time, your default leave time, and the time you arrived at your workplace
- **App settings** — the character you selected and the name you gave it, your character collection, whether haptic feedback is enabled, and whether you have completed onboarding

The App **never collects** information that could identify you, such as your name, email address, phone number, date of
birth, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier (IDFA).

## 2. Use of Location

The App uses location for two purposes only.

1. **Registering a workplace** — showing your current location on the map so you can pin your workplace easily.
2. **Detecting arrival at your workplace** — sending a notification asking "What time are you leaving today?" when you enter the radius of your registered workplace.

Because arrival detection has to work while the App is closed, it relies on iOS geofencing, which requires the
**"Always"** location permission.
Geofence evaluation is handled by iOS on your device, and **the App does not collect or store your movement history or
location log.** The only things stored are the single set of coordinates you pinned yourself and the time you arrived
there today.

If you decline the location permission, every other feature of the App — setting your leave time manually, the timer,
widgets, and notifications — still works as usual.

## 3. Notifications, Widgets, and Live Activities

- All notifications are **local notifications** generated on your device. No remote push server is used.
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities share data with the App through an App Group in order to display the leave time and character information stored on your device. This sharing happens entirely within your device.

## 4. Storage and Retention

- All information is stored in app-private storage (an App Group) protected by iOS.
- There is no separate retention period; information stays on your device until you delete it.
- **Deleting the App deletes all stored information along with it.**
- Today's leave time and arrival time are no longer used once the date changes.

## 5. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not provide the information it collects to third parties, nor does it outsource processing of that
information to anyone.
It uses no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, and no crash reporting tools.

The one exception is the map shown when you register a workplace, which uses Apple's Maps (MapKit). Requests needed to
render the map may therefore be sent to Apple.
This is handled by Apple at the operating system level; see the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

## 6. In-App Purchases and Advertising

The App currently offers no in-app purchases and no advertising, and it collects no payment information.
If in-app purchases are introduced in the future, payment will be handled by Apple's App Store, and the developer will
neither see nor store payment details such as card numbers.

## 7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You can review and delete the information stored on your device at any time.

- **Delete a workplace** — remove a registered workplace from Settings → Workplace.
- **Delete a leave time** — clear today's leave time from the home screen.
- **Withdraw permissions** — turn off location and notification permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app under 집가고싶다.
- **Delete everything** — deleting the App removes all information from your device along with it.

## 8. Security Measures

Because the App does not transmit information externally, there is no risk of interception in transit.
Information stored on your device is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption, and other apps cannot
access it.

## 9. Children Under 14

The App is not directed at children under the age of 14, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from
children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, we will communicate the changes through an app update.
For significant changes, we will also notify you separately within the App.
