**WannaGoHome** (the "App") values your privacy.
The App **has no sign-up and does not operate any servers.** All information created in the App is stored only on your device and is never sent outside of it, including to the developer.

---

## 1. Information We Use

The App stores and uses the following information **only on your device**.

- **Workplace** — the name, latitude and longitude, and arrival detection radius of the workplace you choose on the map
- **Leave times** — today's leave time, your default leave time, and the time you arrived at your workplace
- **App settings** — your selected character and any name you gave it, the characters you own, display language, whether haptic feedback is on, and whether you have completed the first-launch guide (onboarding)

The App **never collects** information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier (IDFA).

## 2. Use of Location Information

The App uses location information for only two purposes.

1. **Setting your workplace** — to show your current location on the map so you can easily choose your workplace.
2. **Detecting arrival at your workplace** — to send a notification asking "What time are you leaving today?" when you enter the radius around your workplace.

Because arrival detection needs to work even while the App is closed, the App uses the iOS geofencing feature and asks for the **"Always"** location permission.
Geofence checks are handled by iOS on your device, and **the App does not collect or store your movements or location history.** The only things stored are the single workplace location you chose yourself and the time you arrived there today.

Even if you do not allow location access, you can still use the rest of the App (setting your leave time manually, the timer, widgets, and notifications).

## 3. Notifications, Widgets, and Live Activities

- All notifications are **local notifications** created on your device. The App does not use a remote push server.
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities share data with the App through an App Group in order to display your leave time and character. This sharing happens only on your device.

## 4. Where Information Is Stored and for How Long

- All information is stored in an App-only storage area (App Group) protected by iOS.
- There is no fixed retention period; information stays on your device until you delete it.
- **Deleting the App deletes all stored information along with it.**
- Today's leave time and arrival time are no longer used once the day changes.

## 5. Sharing with Third Parties and Outsourcing

The App does not share the information it uses with third parties or outsource its processing.
The App does not use any analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools.

However, the map shown when you choose your workplace uses Apple's Maps (MapKit), so requests needed to draw the map may be sent to Apple.
This is handled by Apple at the operating system level. For details, please see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## 6. In-App Purchases and Advertising

The App currently offers no in-app purchases or advertising and does not collect payment information.
If in-app purchases are introduced in the future, payments will be processed by Apple's App Store, and the developer will not see or keep your card number or other payment details.

## 7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You can review and delete the information stored on your device at any time.

- **Delete your workplace** — you can remove your registered workplace in Settings → Workplace.
- **Delete your leave time** — you can clear today's leave time on the home screen.
- **Withdraw permissions** — you can turn off location and notification permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app → WannaGoHome.
- **Delete everything** — deleting the App removes all information from your device.

## 8. Security Measures

Because the App never sends information off your device, there is no risk of leaks during transmission.
Information stored on your device is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption, and other apps cannot access it.

## 9. Children Under 14

The App is not directed at children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will let you know through an App update.
For significant changes, we will also notify you separately within the App.
