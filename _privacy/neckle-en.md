Neckle (the "Service") treats your personal information with care and complies with applicable laws.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide its neck exercise features, the Service uses **head motion data** from the motion sensors in your AirPods.
This data is processed **entirely on your device** for the sole purpose of recognizing exercise movements. It is not
linked to your identity, and it is never transmitted to or stored on any external server.

## 2. Purpose of Use

- Recognizing neck exercise movements and guiding you through a session (processed on device)

The Service does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect personally identifiable information
such as your name or contact details.

## 3. Retention and Deletion

Head motion data is used only for real-time processing during an exercise session and is not retained afterwards.
App settings (such as the record of a connected pair of AirPods) are stored only on your device and are deleted
together with the app when you uninstall it.
